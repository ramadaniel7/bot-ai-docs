Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

At Loonareen Studios, we are committed to respecting your digital privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the minimal data our AI Bot processes and how we ensure your interactions remain secure, private, and fully transparent.

01 Data We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. The only data our Bot processes includes:

  • Discord User IDs & Usernames: To distinguish who is communicating with the AI.
  • Message Prompts: The text queries you send directly to the bot so the AI can understand and answer them.
  • Discord Channel & Guild IDs: To deliver responses to the correct channel within your server.

02 How We Process Your Data

Your queries are processed in **real-time** using the Google Gemini API (the core engine behind our AI).

User Prompt -> Secure API Call -> Gemini AI Inference -> AI Response -> Discord Chat

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal data, usernames, or chat logs with third-party advertising companies or brokers.

03 Data Retention

Our AI Bot is completely **stateless**. Once the response is generated and sent back to your Discord channel, your prompt and the AI's response are immediately cleared from our active server memory. We do not maintain long-term databases of your chat logs.

04 Security Measures

All communications between Discord, our backend hosting at HeavenCloud, and the Google Gemini API are fully encrypted in transit. Access tokens and API keys are stored securely using strict environment configurations to prevent unauthorized access.

05 Your Rights & Deletion

Since we do not store chat logs, your data is inherently ephemeral. If you wish to clear past interactions from Discord's UI, you can manually delete your messages directly in your Discord channel.
